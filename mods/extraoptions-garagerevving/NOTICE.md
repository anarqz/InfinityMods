# Extra Options - Garage Revving: attribution and scope

Garage engine revving: Arthur (RokkuDayo), part of Extra Options,
https://github.com/RokkuDayo/ForzaHorizon2_ExtraOptions, GPL-3.0 (LICENSE.txt). Built unchanged from
upstream's source (`fh2/plugins/garage-engine-rev`), at the exact commit of the `upstream/extraoptions`
submodule of https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityMods at the commit this package was built from, with Zig's
Clang and the flags of upstream's own `build.py`; only the version resource is Infinity's.

ExtraOptions' mod loader (built into Infinity as ExtraOptions.Infinity) loads `plugins\EO_GarageEngineRev.dll`
from this folder (`<Azimuth>\mods\Forza Horizon 2\extraoptions-garagerevving\`). Hold RT in the garage to hear
the car's engine rev. Nothing other players see, hear or race against: it plays in every mode.
